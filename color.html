Locked Palette (Names + Roles)

These are the colors you "lock" across the whole product suite.
Brand Core
Usage rules (keep it premium):
• Backgrounds: space + panel only.
• Text: only text-*. Avoid pure white everywhere.
• Accent (Spark): max 5-10% of UI. Only CTAs, badges, key highlights.
• Cyan/Blue/Violet: focus rings, links, subtle highlights, "tech glow" - not big surfaces.

VR Glow / Motion Tuning

Modern "futuristic" feel comes from glow + motion restraint.
Effects
Glow formula (recommended)
• Primary buttons: --glow-primary
• Accent CTAs / sale badges: --glow-accent
• Brand moments (hero, spotlight): --glow-cyan

Keep glow soft: opacity 0.10-0.30, blur radius 18-70px.
Store
Immersive glow (cyan + spark)
Admin
Minimal glow (clarity first)
Dev
Creative glow (violet + spark)
Motion rule: use micro-motion only (120-200ms). No bouncing everywhere.
Hover: translateY(-1px) + small glow increase. Tap: translateY(+1px).
This keeps it "fun" while staying professional.
